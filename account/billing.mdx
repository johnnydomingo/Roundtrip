Roundtrip is billed per workspace, based on the plan you choose and the number
of seats attached to it.

## Plans

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="rocket">
    Local runs only. No query console, no shared workspaces.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team" icon="circle-user">
    Shared workspaces, the query console, and CI reporters. Billed monthly per seat.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Upgrading or downgrading takes effect immediately. You're charged a prorated
  amount for the rest of the current billing cycle.
</Note>

## Usage metering

Two things count against your plan's limits: test runs and query console
queries. Both reset at the start of each billing cycle.

<ResponseField name="test runs" type="count">
  Every invocation of `roundtrip run`, regardless of how many test files it
  includes, counts as one run.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="queries" type="count">
  Each `query` block executed against a connected database counts as one
  query, whether it runs standalone or as part of a test.
</ResponseField>

See exact thresholds on the [limits](/customers/limits) page.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first of each month and sent to the workspace's
billing contact. Past invoices are available from the dashboard under
**Settings → Billing**.

<Card title="Manage billing" icon="credit-card" href="https://app.mintlify.com">
  Update your plan, seats, or payment method.
</Card>

## Questions

<Accordion title="Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?">
  Yes, from **Settings → Billing**. Annual plans are billed upfront and prorate
  any remaining monthly balance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan's limits?">
  Test runs and queries still execute, but you'll be prompted to upgrade
  before your next cycle starts. We don't cut off traffic mid-cycle.
</Accordion>
